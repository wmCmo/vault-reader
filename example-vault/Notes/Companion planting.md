## Pairs that work

| Plant | Good with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tomato | Basil | Basil is said to deter pests |
| Carrot | Onion | Each masks the other's scent |
| Lettuce | Radish | Radish is harvested before lettuce needs the room |

## Pairs to avoid

- Tomato and fennel
- Beans and onions

> [!question] Is any of this proven?
> Some of it is folklore. Treat it as a cheap experiment.
