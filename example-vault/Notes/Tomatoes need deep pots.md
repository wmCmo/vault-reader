Part of [[🌿 Garden Planner]].

Tomato roots go deep. In a 10 L pot the plant stalls by midsummer; **20 L or more** keeps it fruiting into autumn.

> [!warning] Shallow pots dry out fast
> On a sunny balcony a small pot can dry out in a single afternoon. See [[Watering schedule]].

| Pot size | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 10 L | Stunted, few fruit |
| 20 L | Healthy |
| 30 L+ | Best, but heavy |
