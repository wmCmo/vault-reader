> [!quote] On systems (my paraphrase)
> Goals set the direction. Systems decide how far you actually get.

The useful idea: ==make the habit obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying==.[^1] Each one is a lever you can pull without relying on willpower.

## Notes

- Identity first: "I'm someone who reads" beats "I want to read 20 books".
- Habit stacking: *after I pour coffee, I read one page*.
- Never miss twice.

[^1]: The four laws of behaviour change, from chapter 3.
