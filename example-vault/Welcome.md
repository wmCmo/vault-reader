This is a **demo vault** for Vault Reader. Everything here renders the way Obsidian shows it, from a copy stored on the phone.

> [!tip] Read anywhere
> The phone keeps the whole vault. On a plane, in a basement, or when your VPN acts up, every note still opens. It syncs again the moment your PC is reachable.

## What's in here

- [[🌿 Garden Planner]]: a hub note whose list is built by a Dataview query
- [[🗾 京都 2026]]: properties, tables and Japanese text
- [[Atomic Habits]]: quotes, ==highlights== and footnotes
- [[Snippets]]: syntax-highlighted code
- [[Ideas.canvas|Ideas board]]: a canvas, shown as cards
